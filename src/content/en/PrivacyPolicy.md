# Powerwise – Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how De Digitale Energiecoach B.V. (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects personal data when individuals use the Powerwise app. It describes what information we collect, why we collect it, how we store it, and how users can exercise their privacy rights.

We may update this policy from time to time to comply with legal requirements or to reflect changes in our processes or features. We will notify you by making the latest version always available in the app and on our website. If you have any questions or want to exercise your rights, please contact [privacy@dedigitaleenergiecoach.nl](mailto:privacy@dedigitaleenergiecoach.nl).

## 1. Who We Are

Powerwise is operated by De Digitale Energiecoach B.V., based in the Netherlands. We act as the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation.

## 2. What Data We Process

Powerwise applies data minimization as a design principle. No personal data is processed without a clear and necessary use case. We process:

- Account data: email address and house address
- Household data (optional): age, gender, and number of residents
- Usage data: AI chat interactions, session duration, and progress tracking

Users can review and correct household or home-related data in the app if it is inaccurate.

## 3. How We Use Data

We use the data to:

- Provide and enhance app functionality (habit tracking, streaks, AI suggestions)
- Measure and improve user engagement
- Create anonymized and aggregated reports for municipalities
- Implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk

## 4. Legal Basis

We process data because it is necessary for:

- Providing the service (consent)
- Improving the app and ensuring security (legitimate interest)

## 5. AI Use

Powerwise integrates with OpenAI to generate suggestions. We do not send your personal data to OpenAI. If you include any personal information in a chat with the AI, such as your full name, it may be processed by OpenAI to generate a response. Your data is not used for AI model training.

## 6. Third Party Processors

We use the services of the following third parties to provide the app to you:

- Google Cloud Platform (data hosting)
- Neon (database management and analytics)
- Supabase (authentication)
- OpenAI (to generate AI-based suggestions)

We do not sell user data or share it for marketing and/or advertising purposes.

## 7. Data Retention

- User data: kept as long as your account is active
- Usage data: kept up to 36 months from your last activity

You can delete your account at any time. Deletion removes personal and household-level data, but anonymized data remains. Aggregated and anonymized data may be stored longer for analytics.

## 8. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored on EU servers. Taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, the nature, scope, context, and purposes of processing, as well as the risk to the rights and freedoms of individuals, we implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure secure handling of your data.

## 9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Request access to your data
- Correct or delete your data
- Restrict or object to processing
- Withdraw consent (if applicable)
- Submit a complaint with a supervisory authority in your country
