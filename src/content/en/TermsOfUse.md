## 1. About Us

Powerwise is developed by De Digitale Energiecoach B.V., a company registered in the Netherlands. The app helps users reduce energy costs by tracking household habits and suggesting ways to improve energy efficiency.

## 2. What Powerwise Does

Powerwise allows users to log energy-related behaviors, set energy-saving actions, track progress, and receive AI-based suggestions. The goal is to support users in understanding and reducing their energy consumption. The app is inspired by the work of real-life energy coaches in the Netherlands but does not replace them.

## 3. Who Can Use the App

The app is intended for residents in municipalities that use Powerwise. Any resident can use it, including minors. Users under 16 should obtain consent from a parent or guardian.

## 4. Accounts

To use Powerwise, users create an account with an email address and house address.

During onboarding, users may optionally share information related to the household such as age, gender, budget, motivation for using Powerwise, and household size, and information related to the energy-related features of the house, such as insulation, solar panels, and other installations that affect energy use. We also use publicly available information about the house, such as square meters, energy label, construction year, building type, and comparable data from public registers and open data sources.
